Max Verstapen

Max Emilian Verstappen is a Belgian-Dutch Formula One racing driver competing for Red Bull Racing under the Dutch flag.

Key Details:

  • Born: September 30, 1997 (Hasselt, Belgium)
  • Nationality: Belgian-Dutch
  • F1 Team: Oracle Red Bull Racing (Car #3)
  • Championships: 4-time consecutive Formula One World Champion (2021, 2022, 2023, 2024)

F1 Career Stats:

Max Verstappen burst onto the Formula One scene as a prodigy, rewriting the history books almost immediately. He became the youngest driver ever to start an F1 race and the youngest to win one, capturing a legendary victory at the 2016 Spanish Grand Prix when he was just 18 years old. This sensational debut signaled the arrival of a generational talent destined to challenge the sport's elite.

His raw speed quickly evolved into ruthless consistency, leading to an era of absolute dominance with Oracle Red Bull Racing. Verstappen reached the pinnacle of motorsport by securing four consecutive Formula One World Championships from 2021 through 2024. His relentless driving style and tactical brilliance cemented his status as the undisputed force on the modern grid.

During this championship run, he shattered records that many believed were untouchable. His historic 2023 season stands as the most dominant campaign in F1 history, highlighted by 19 individual race victories and an unprecedented streak of 10 consecutive wins. With over 70 career victories before turning 30, he has firmly established himself among the greatest drivers to ever play the sport.
